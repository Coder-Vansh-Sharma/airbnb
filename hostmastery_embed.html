📸 The Golden 5-Photo Hero Order

            
On mobile, 80% of guests decide whether to tap based on the first 5 photos:

            

              

                Photo 1 (Hero Cover): Bright living room angle with view or stunning exterior shot. Must have crisp daylight and no wide-angle blur.
              

              

                Photo 2 (Master Bedroom): Freshly pressed white linens, clean pillows, bedside lamps, showing sleeping comfort clearly.
              

              

                Photo 3 (The Standout Angle): Private balcony with chai setup, mountain/pool view, or cozy reading nook.
              

              

                Photo 4 (Spotless Bathroom): Demonstrates hygiene, geyser/hot water, dry floor, fresh towels and toiletries.
              

              

                Photo 5 (Workation / Kitchen): Shows dedicated desk with Wi-Fi speed test or fully equipped kitchen for self-cooking.
              

            

          


          

            
⚠️ Top 3 Mistakes of Indian Homestays

            
    
              
  • Overpromising Wi-Fi: Calling a 10 Mbps connection "Ultra High Speed 200 Mbps" leads to immediate 1-star reviews from remote workers.
    • 
              
  • Hiding Constraints: Not mentioning that the property is on the 3rd floor without a lift. Always state stair counts upfront.
    • 
              
  • Ambiguous Visitor Rules: Clearly disclose whether unregistered local friends are allowed in the room or only in the common lounge.

Knowledge · Academy · Best Practices

          
Learn & Improve (Host Academy)

          
Clear, practical guides on AI prompt engineering for hosts, smartphone photography, handling difficult guests, and Indian homestay regulations.

        

      


      

        
        

          
Module 01

          
AI Prompting Masterclass for Hosts

          
How to get genuine, high-converting copy from ChatGPT or Gemini instead of generic travel fluff:

          

            
1

            
Give Property Facts First: Always specify exact room dimensions, real bed sizes, and verified distances. Never let AI guess.

          

          

            
2

            
Specify the Indian Context: Instruct: "Write in warm, hospitable Indian English (or Hinglish) suitable for families and young travelers."

          

          

            
3

            
Forbid Unverified Superlatives: Explicitly tell the AI: "Do not use words like 'luxurious', 'best in town', or 'hidden gem' unless substantiated."

          

        


        
        

          
Module 02

          
Smartphone Photography & Staging

          
You don't need a ₹1,00,000 camera to win bookings. Follow these smartphone rules:

          

            
✓

            
Turn on All Warm Lights: Switch on bedside lamps and ceiling fixtures even during the daytime for a warm, cozy glow.

          

          

            
✓

            
Shoot from Waist Height: Holding the phone at chest/waist level keeps vertical wall lines straight and rooms looking natural.

          

          

            
✓

            
Avoid Ultra-Wide Distortion: 0.5x ultra-wide makes rooms look unnaturally huge, leading to guest disappointment and bad reviews. Use 1x and step back.

          

        


        
        

          
Module 03

          
Handling Difficult Hosting Realities

          
How to handle unexpected issues calmly and protect your 5-star rating:

          

            
⚡

            
Sudden Power Cuts: Message the guest immediately before they message you. Inform them about inverter capacity and estimated restoration time.

          

          

            
⚡

            
Late Check-in Demands: Install a simple digital key lockbox so guests arriving at 2:00 AM can self check-in seamlessly.

          

          

            
⚡

            
Quiet Hour Violations: Have your on-site caretaker offer a gentle verbal reminder rather than an aggressive confrontation.

          

        


        
        

          
Module 04

          
Indian Homestay Regulations & Trust

          
Legal & regulatory essentials for peaceful hosting in India:

          

            
📋

            
Government ID Verification: Collect Aadhaar / Passport details via a secure digital form prior to or upon physical check-in.

          

          

            
📋

            
C-Form for Foreign Guests: Mandatory registration on the Indian Bureau of Immigration BoI portal within 24 hours of arrival.

          

          

            
📋

            
Fire Safety & First Aid: Keep an active ABC dry-powder fire extinguisher and stocked first-aid kit visibly accessible.
